Lead playbook

Our story

The missing middle in commercial real estate

FillSpace was born from one observation: owners lose months of revenue waiting for long-term tenants, while startups are boxed into inflexible leases. We built the operating system between those two extremes.

How it works

A simple 4-step booking experience

1

Search & Request

Browse curated listings by location, size, price, and amenities. Submit a booking request with your business details and intended timeline.

2

Get Approved

Property owners review your application within 48 hours. FillSpace handles credit checks and business verification to reduce back-and-forth.

3

Sign & Pay

Review and e-sign your agreement in-platform. Make secure payment and activate insurance coverage built directly into the booking flow.

4

Move In & Grow

Get your keys and launch quickly. After your initial term, maintain flexibility with a 30-day rolling notice model.
