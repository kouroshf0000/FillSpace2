Our story

The missing middle in commercial real estate

Owners wait months to fill vacancies while growth-stage businesses are pushed into rigid leases. FillSpace bridges that gap with flexible mid-term rentals.

How it works

A simple 4-step booking experience

1

Search & Request

Browse curated listings and submit a booking request with your business details.

2

Get Approved

Owners review your request in up to 48 hours. FillSpace handles verification and checks.

3

Sign & Pay

Review and e-sign your agreement, then complete secure payment with insurance included.

4

Move In & Grow

Move in and launch. After the initial term, continue with 30-day rolling notice.
